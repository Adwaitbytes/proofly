How it works

Proof-based compliance, in four moving parts.

01

Verify once

Identity and financials checked a single time. Documents stay in a user-controlled vault.

02

Seal into a passport

Bound to you by a deterministic hash. Unlinkable across institutions.

03

Prove on demand

The agent returns a zero-knowledge proof, only true or false, never the data.

04

Monitor continuously

Scores every transaction and pauses risk before settlement, with a full audit trail.
