How it works

Proof-based compliance, in four moving parts

A real sequence: verify, seal, prove, watch. Each step is enforced by cryptography and an autonomous agent, not by trust.

Step 01

Verify once

Identity and financials are checked a single time. Documents stay in a user-controlled vault, never re-shared.

Step 02

Seal into a passport

Credentials are bound to you by a deterministic hash. The same person always maps to the same passport, unlinkable across dApps.

Step 03

Prove on demand

The agent reads each institution's request and returns a zero-knowledge proof, only true or false, never the underlying data.

Step 04

Monitor continuously

Post-onboarding, the agent scores every transaction and pauses fraud or sanctions risk before settlement, with a full audit trail.
